# Privacy Policy – OpenRZ67

_Last updated: 2026-09-26_

OpenRZ67 does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- The app has no internet access, no analytics, no ads and no accounts.
- Bluetooth is used only to find and connect to the openrz67-trigger remote. The Bluetooth scan permission is declared with `neverForLocation`, and the app does not use your location.
- The only data the app sends is shutter commands and their timing (e.g. countdown or bulb duration) over Bluetooth to the connected openrz67-trigger. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere else.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/openrz67/openrz67-android/issues>.
